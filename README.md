🚀 NextCamp — The Campus That Fixes Itself

NextCamp is a campus management platform designed to make everyday campus life smarter, simpler, and more efficient. It aims to bridge the gap between students, campus facilities, and administration by providing a centralized digital platform for campus-related needs.

Built during Gradient Rush, an overnight hackathon organized by Reinforce × OSC at Scaler School of Technology.

💡 The Idea

College campuses face everyday challenges involving communication, coordination, and managing student needs. NextCamp envisions a smarter campus where problems can be reported, information can be accessed easily, and campus operations can become more efficient.

Our vision is simple:

A campus that doesn't just manage problems — it fixes itself.

✨ Key Highlights

🏫 A centralized platform for campus-related activities.

📢 A digital space to improve campus communication.

🛠️ An approach towards smarter campus problem-solving.

💻 A simple and accessible web-based interface.
